# TinyRime v0.1 research release report

Release status: **research-complete, publication-ready subject to manual GitHub push**.

This release closes the corrected 100k first-sweep study. It does not start a new training direction, expand the dataset, or make a product-readiness claim.

## What v0.1 accomplished

1. Removed the retained-success first-K bias from validation and test evaluation.
2. Decoupled complete train candidate generation from deterministic 100k sampling.
3. Persisted one canonical contested flag defined on the fixed query pool.
4. Added split-specific, deterministic miss diagnostics.
5. Replaced scalar-hashed candidate type with a categorical encoding path while retaining an explicit compatibility mode for old checkpoints.
6. Audited hash collisions and added exact-vocabulary support.
7. Froze the immutable `TinyRime-Context-v1` benchmark manifest.
8. Re-evaluated Rime, Wanxiang, MLP, and Tiny-2M/4M/8M on every recallable validation and test example.
9. Exported a full sample-level prediction artifact on AutoDL and derived overlap, oracle, contested, grouped-error, and val-selected hybrid metrics.
10. Chose one focused research direction and stopped without additional training.

The old first-10k pilot overstated all conditional Top-1 scores: Rime moved from 84.050% to 83.451%, Wanxiang from 88.470% to 87.944%, and Tiny-8M from 87.140% to 86.621%. Model ordering did not change. Tiny-8M remains the best neural model; Wanxiang remains the best standalone method.

Against Rime, Tiny-8M has 717 wins, 17 losses, and +700 net wins. Against Wanxiang, Tiny-8M has 633 wins, 925 losses, and -292 net wins. It therefore does not exceed Wanxiang as a standalone reranker.

## Wanxiang × Tiny-8M overlap and oracle

| Test quadrant | Examples | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Both correct | 18,493 | 83.755% |
| Wanxiang only correct | 925 | 4.189% |
| Tiny-8M only correct | 633 | 2.867% |
| Both wrong | 2,029 | 9.189% |

The Wanxiang/Tiny-8M oracle is **90.811% Top-1**, a **+2.867 percentage-point** ceiling over Wanxiang. On the 11,106 canonical contested examples, Wanxiang scores 80.083%, Tiny-8M 77.598%, and the oracle 84.702%; the contested quadrants are 8,105 both correct, 789 Wanxiang-only, 513 Tiny-only, and 1,699 both wrong.

The simple existing router selects its threshold on validation only. On test it reaches **90.349% Top-1**, 83.766% contested Top-1, 4.375% promotion coverage, 697 wins, 166 losses, +531 net wins, and 72.153% promotion precision. A stricter val-selected operating point targeting at least 95% validation precision reaches 88.401% test Top-1 with 103 wins, 2 losses, +101 net wins, 0.476% coverage, and 98.095% realized test promotion precision.

These hybrid numbers are evidence that routing is promising. They are not a multi-seed result, a calibrated production policy, or evidence on user typing.

## Error-type findings

- The two disagreement directions are dominated by two-character targets: 899/925 Wanxiang-only cases and 618/633 Tiny-only cases.
- Wanxiang-only cases are mostly targets originally at Rime rank 1 or 2 (595 and 168 cases). Wanxiang is particularly effective at selecting an alternative near the head of the list.
- Tiny-only cases include 227 examples where original Rime rank 0 was already correct, versus only 15 such Wanxiang-only cases. A useful router must detect when Wanxiang would damage an already-correct Rime choice.
- Most examples have 17–32 characters of context. In that dominant bucket, Wanxiang is stronger (88.064% versus Tiny-8M 86.597%).
- Tiny-8M is stronger in the 0–4 context bucket (84.737% versus 77.895%), but that bucket has only 190 examples and cannot support a broad claim.
- On the 1,775 high-ambiguity examples, Wanxiang is also stronger (89.127% versus 86.535%).

## What can and cannot be claimed

Supported:

- Tiny-8M materially improves over raw Rime on `TinyRime-Context-v1`.
- Wanxiang is stronger than every standalone learned reranker reproduced here.
- Tiny-8M and Wanxiang have measurable complementary errors.
- A validation-selected hybrid exceeds Wanxiang on this single frozen test set.
- Tiny-8M's FP16 weight file is about 3.54% of the external Wanxiang grammar file's size.

Not supported:

- best open-source Chinese IME;
- superiority on real typing or any external benchmark;
- multi-seed stability, 300k/1M scaling, or optimal hyperparameters;
- product latency, RSS, Core ML, Squirrel, or full macOS input-method readiness;
- public redistribution of the trained checkpoint.

## Why no residual training was run

The corrected overlap result already answers the release decision: standalone scaling is not the immediate bottleneck, while an existing val-selected router recovers most of the oracle opportunity without new training. Starting another 100k experiment would broaden the release and blur whether gains came from routing, calibration, or model retraining. v0.1 therefore stops here.

## The next single research question

Can a validation-calibrated residual router predict **when Wanxiang is wrong and Tiny-8M is right**, while preserving at least 95% promotion precision across seeds? This is the only recommended next experiment. It should compare against the existing threshold router before any data scaling, larger model, distillation, or candidate-pool expansion.
